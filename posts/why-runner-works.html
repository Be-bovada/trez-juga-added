MLB Power Rankings: Post-Trade Deadline Edition

Weekly updates on the best teams in baseball.

Published October 28, 2025

The trade deadline has passed, and the contenders have been separated from the pretenders. With two months left in the regular season, the pennant races are heating up. In this edition of our power rankings, we evaluate every team based on their current roster, recent performance, and strength of schedule down the stretch.

1. The Unstoppable Force

This team did not stand pat at the deadline, adding a key bullpen arm and a veteran bat. Their starting rotation is the best in baseball, and their lineup has no easy outs. They are the clear World Series favorites.

2. The Surprise Contender

Few predicted this team would be in the hunt, but a combination of breakout performances from young players and savvy coaching has them sitting atop their division. Can they keep it up in September?

3. The Sleeping Giant

Plagued by injuries in the first half, this team is finally getting healthy. They have the talent to go on a major run, but their hole in the standings might be too deep to dig out of. They are the ultimate wild card.
