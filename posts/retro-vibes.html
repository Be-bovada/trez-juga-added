Fantasy Football: Breakout Stars for the Second Half

Win your league with these waiver-wire heroes.

Published October 29, 2025

Championships aren't won at the draft. They are won on the waiver wire and by identifying breakout stars before anyone else. As we hit the midpoint of the season, several players are seeing their roles expand due to injuries or improved play. These are the players you need to target to make a championship push.

1. The Rookie Receiver

After a quiet start to the season, this first-round pick is starting to look like the real deal. His target share has increased in three straight weeks, and he is developing a strong rapport with his quarterback. His schedule gets much easier down the stretch. Grab him now before he has a monster game.

2. The Handcuff Running Back

The starter on his team has been dealing with nagging injuries all year, and this backup has looked explosive in limited action. If the starter misses time, this player immediately becomes a top-10 fantasy running back. He's the most valuable insurance policy in fantasy football.
