The Art of Tailgating: A Fan's Guide

How to host the perfect pre-game party.

Published November 1, 2025

The game is only part of the game day experience. A great tailgate can set the tone for a memorable day, win or lose. But what separates a good tailgate from a legendary one? It's all in the preparation. Here is our ultimate guide to becoming a parking lot hero.

1. The Menu

Keep it simple but delicious. Burgers and hot dogs are classics for a reason. Prepare as much as you can at home to minimize work at the stadium. Don't forget the essentials: buns, condiments, and plenty of napkins.

2. The Setup

A portable grill, a cooler packed with ice, and comfortable chairs are non-negotiable. A pop-up tent is a pro move for shade or rain protection. And of course, bring a football to toss around.

3. The Vibe

A great playlist is key. Represent your team with flags and banners. Most importantly, be a good neighbor. The tailgate is a community, so be friendly to the fans around you, even if they're wearing the wrong colors.
